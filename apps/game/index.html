How to play
Controls: put your left thumb anywhere and drag to move & circle the octagon (up/down = step sideways – use it to get off the cage). JAB fast · CROSS strong straight – hold to charge a power shot · L KICK low kick: can't be ducked, hurts the legs and slows them down · R KICK head kick, longest reach – hold to charge · DUCK under jabs, crosses and head kicks, then counter · BLOCK hold · SHOOT for a takedown – DUCK as he shoots to sprawl. On the ground you both pick a move each exchange: top Pass / Strike / Control / Stand up, bottom Frame / Cover / Sweep / Get up. Charging is risky: get hit while winding up and it counts as a counter. Hitting someone while they are winding up or recovering is a counter (+40 %). Every attack costs stamina – when you're gassed you get slower and weaker. Big shots can leave the opponent rocked – and every clean shot to the head carries a small chance of a flash knockdown, much bigger for counters, charged power shots and head kicks. Faded buttons = out of range. 3 rounds; KO, TKO (3 knockdowns) or the judges decide.
Keyboard: WASD move, J jab, K cross, U low kick, I head kick (hold K/I to charge), Space duck, L block, O shoot (ground: J K U I).
